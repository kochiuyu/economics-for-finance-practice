Case seminar · Dianrong

Should the lending platform guarantee repayment?

Decide whether taking credit risk earns enough extra fee income to justify the funding and capital required.

The original course assigned Dianrong: Marketplace Lending, Blockchain, and “The New Finance” in China. Students considered expansion along the lending value chain and how to finance it. The following independent exercise uses a fictional platform. Published case, HBS 218043

Your decision. Keep a fee-only platform or guarantee lenders against principal losses? A guarantee attracts lenders, but your platform must now finance the losses it promises to cover.

Per cohort, illustrative units Fee only Guarantee
Funded loans 80 100
Fee per funded loan 60 90
Screening and operating costs 2,400 3,000
Cost of maintaining committed capital 0 500

Each loan has principal of 1,000. If it defaults, recovery is 40% of principal. Under the guarantee the platform pays the remaining 600. Under fee-only lending the lender bears that loss. Ignore discounting and assume all stated fees are collected.

Vote before reviewing the credit forecast

Fee-only contribution is 80 × 60 − 2,400 = 2,400. Before credit losses the guarantee earns 100 × 90 − 3,000 − 500 = 5,500. How many defaults would consume the entire incremental contribution?

Set the expansion threshold

The guarantee adds 3,100 before losses. At five defaults, it pays 3,000 and earns 2,500, just 100 more than fee only. Six defaults reduce contribution to 1,900. The break-even expected default rate is 3,100 / (100 × 600) = 5.17%. At 4% expected defaults, guarantee contribution is 3,100. At 8% it is 700.

Fund the promise

Suppose only 4,000 in cash is available when claims fall due. Eight defaults require 4,800 in payouts. An 800 liquidity shortfall can arise even though fees over the cohort leave positive contribution. A board approval should therefore specify both the expected default rate and the cash available in a correlated default episode. Compare loss rates by origination cohort and verify the timing of recoveries before relying on the threshold.
