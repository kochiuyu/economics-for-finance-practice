The original course

Economics for Finance Practice was a required course for part-time Master of Finance students at The Chinese University of Hong Kong.

Instructor: Chiu Yu Ko. Teaching term: second trimester, December 2023 to March 2024. The January 2024 outline described a course spanning microeconomics, macroeconomics, and applications to finance. Students used models in quizzes, group case discussions, and a cumulative examination.

Assessment

Component Weight Format
Participation 5% Contribution to class discussion
Quizzes 20% Two multiple-choice quizzes
Group project 30% Case report, presentation, and discussion
Final exam 45% Cumulative examination with multiple-choice questions and an applied micro case

The final exam used a Hong Kong waste-charging scenario. A separate sample exercise asked students to interpret co-movement in Shenzhen property prices and IPO activity. The public edition uses that second exercise as the starting point for its planned evidence workshops and capstone.

About this edition

The 2024 syllabus, numbered lecture files, and case question pack establish the original course record. Earlier drafts contain additional portfolio and behavioral material. The public edition expands these into dedicated chapters. Its interactive exercises and illustrative datasets are identified on the relevant pages.
